    Binary survival classification using data from Hospital Arnaldo Milian Castro. The study compared logistic regression, KNN and multilayer perceptrons with Random Oversampling and SMOTE, hyperparameter optimization, and evaluation using F1, recall and AUC-ROC. It reported the best results with a multilayer perceptron after Random Oversampling and used LIME for local explanations.

03 / Background

Mathematical roots.
Software foundations.

I am a PhD student at the University of Granada and a researcher at DaSCI. My work connects explainable AI and language models with a background in distributed optimization and anomaly detection.

Alongside research, my experience includes REST API development, data processing, and university teaching. I am interested in applied AI/ML, research engineering, and data science roles that turn experiments into useful software.

I also use OpenCode as a coding agent, together with Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, reusable skills and agent harnesses, as part of my development workflow.
